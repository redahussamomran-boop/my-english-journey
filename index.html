CEFR Course Library · 100 lessons

A sequenced A1–C1 course with 20 lessons per level, explicit grammar, reading/listening, vocabulary, writing, quizzes, and classic stories including Shakespeare. The CEFR does not prescribe a fixed lesson count. Cambridge estimates around 700–800 guided learning hours from beginner to C1, so this app is a structured practice course—not a claim that 100 short lessons alone certify C1. Each lesson includes browser audio, a reading room, selectable vocabulary, English definitions, example sentences, writing practice, comprehension checks, and level mastery reviews.
